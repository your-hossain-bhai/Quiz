// FAQ

Common questions

How does the 2 BDT/day subscription work?

Enter a Robi or Airtel mobile number, request an OTP, and confirm. QuizHub validates against the BDApps carrier billing API and unlocks the app on the same device.

Why is the Day 1 IQ critical?

Passing the Day 1 IQ test is the first hurdle for many engineering roles in Bangladesh. QuizHub mirrors the verbal and non-verbal question styles you'll see on test day.

How do I sync my subscription in the APK?

Use the same mobile number and BDApps login flow inside the app. The app hits the same PHP backend to verify your subscription before unlocking features.

What if my APK download button doesn't work?

If the APK isn't yet attached, the button will open a panel with build instructions for client_app/. Subscribe still works regardless of APK availability.

Is this app safe?

Yes. The flow uses BDApps carrier billing and mobile verification. No extra payment path is introduced outside the subscription provider.
